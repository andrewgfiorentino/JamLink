# JamLink Contributor Licence Agreement

This document has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It is a working agreement for
a small project, written to keep JamLink's licensing options open. If JamLink
ever takes outside investment, ships commercially, or accepts contributions
from someone's employer, replace it with a reviewed agreement — the Apache
Software Foundation's ICLA and the Harmony agreements are the usual starting
points.

## Why this exists

JamLink is released under GPL-3.0-or-later and has one copyright holder. That
is the only reason a dual-licensed or commercial edition remains possible
later: a sole holder may relicense their own work at any time.

The moment a contribution lands without this agreement, that stops being true.
Relicensing would then require permission from every contributor individually,
forever, including anyone who has moved on or cannot be reached. One merged
patch is enough to close the door permanently.

## What you are agreeing to

By signing, you confirm that:

1. **You wrote it, or you have the right to submit it.** The contribution is
   your original work, or you have permission to submit work you do not own.
2. **Your employer is not going to object.** If you have an employment
   agreement that assigns your work, you have their permission to contribute.
3. **You grant a broad licence.** You grant Andrew Fiorentino a perpetual,
   worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use,
   reproduce, modify, publish, sublicense, and distribute your contribution,
   **including under licence terms other than GPL-3.0-or-later**, such as a
   commercial or proprietary licence.
4. **You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment. You may
   use your own contribution however you like, including in other projects.
5. **You grant a patent licence.** You grant a perpetual, worldwide,
   royalty-free patent licence covering your contribution, for any patents you
   control that it would necessarily infringe.
6. **No warranty.** You provide the contribution as is, with no warranty of any
   kind.

Point 3 is the one that matters, and it is the reason the agreement exists at
all. It is also why a contribution licensed to the project under GPL alone is
not sufficient: GPL does not permit the project to relicense your work.

## What you keep

Your copyright, your name in the git history, and the right to do anything you
want with your own code elsewhere. Nothing here is exclusive.

## How to sign

Add a line to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) in the same pull request as
your first contribution:

```
- Full Name <email@example.com> — I have read and agree to CLA.md (YYYY-MM-DD)
```

Committing that line with your contribution is the signature. Pull requests
without it will be left open rather than merged, and that is not a judgement
on the code.
